What a matched comparison must freeze

  1. Target set and exact statement hashes before the run.
  2. Library snapshot and baseline definition.
  3. Same prover snapshot, per-target time, call count, output-token cap, and spend ceiling.
  4. All attempted items in each denominator, including timeouts, rejected proofs, and blocked runs.
  5. Outcome rule: Lean acceptance of a candidate bound to the exact target and source hash.
