# MathCase public reproduction evidence

## Report an independent check

If you have access to a machine outside the MathCase project, there are three checks you can repeat from public files. A failed run is worth reporting too.

| What you want to check | Where to start | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A small Lean proof | [23-step Collatz proof, exact revision and command](https://github.com/DarthCeltic/MathCase/blob/f6025ba3ca220e5749036976497b71d11852ef29/reproduction/collatz-parity-envelopes-2026-11-v1/evidence/23-14-public-main-2026-11-26/README.md) | Four Lean Core modules compile. For starts at least 3 with at most 14 odd inputs, the 23rd shortcut endpoint lies below the start. |
| A finite arithmetic result, using Python alone | [Smallest possible largest denominators](https://github.com/DarthCeltic/MathCase/tree/6d6791c25dea00d08e3663c0ea3fdbcec6d9dda0/research/number-theory/egyptian-fractions/least-largest-denominator-v1) | The integer checker verifies 143 minima. The unique worst ratio is 1,032 at the prime 9,601. |
| The complete M20 build | [Full source replay instructions](m20-public-source-replay-v1/ORIGINAL_REPLAY.md) | Two runner-bound full source replays completed: 363 modules, 23 axiom groups, and 4,262 declaration queries. The Linux-container run took 3:42:30; the separate native Windows run took 3:51:54.679. Stranger reproduction remains open. |

To [report a replay](https://github.com/DarthCeltic/MathCase/issues/new?title=Independent%20replay), give the case, commit from `git rev-parse HEAD`, OS, tool versions, command and outcome. For a failure, the first failing stage and its diagnostic are especially helpful. Small summaries or public log links are sufficient. A comparison of the two outputs, or a discrepancy you can reproduce, gives readers something concrete to follow.

Mathematicians can also [review a statement](https://github.com/DarthCeltic/MathCase/issues/new?template=mathematical-review.yml). Identify the declaration and the plain-language sentence you compared. Say whether the hypotheses, conclusion, step convention and literature attribution agree. A literature reference, a counterexample or a suggested correction is welcome. A mathematical review can be recorded independently of a software run.

If you tried a question of your own, [report what happened in the visitor lab](https://github.com/DarthCeltic/MathCase/issues/new?template=own-problem.yml). The form asks where your input came from and accepts unsuccessful attempts.

For a short mathematical review, take the [prime denominator criterion](../research/number-theory/egyptian-fractions/prime-envelope-v1/README.md). Does the parity step exclude every competing multiplier under the stated cap? Does the counting argument establish the stated density bound? A prior reference for either claim is welcome.

## Proofs and saved evidence

Start with the [bounded 23/14 Collatz proof](collatz-parity-envelopes-2026-11-v1/README.md). For every start `n ≥ 3` with at most 14 odd inputs in the first 23 accelerated steps, an ordinary iterate is smaller by step 37. Lean counted 7,507,638 qualifying residue classes out of 8,388,608 modulo `2^23`. The package includes the one-command replay and its successful fresh public-checkout receipt.

Visitors can also read the [right-triangle midpoint case](coordinate-right-triangle-midpoint-v1/README.md), including the [general-coordinate theorem](coordinate-right-triangle-midpoint-general-v1/README.md), start with the [six-dot puzzle](https://darthceltic.github.io/mathcase/) or inspect the [saved proof example](https://darthceltic.github.io/mathcase/reader.html?scope=public#case/hero-prime-divisibility-r1), where Lean rejected an attempt and accepted a correction.

Each package below says what question it addresses, what was actually checked, and what remains open. A receipt records the exact files and tools for a run; it does not by itself prove a broader claim or replace a human review of the wording.

- [A finite Ising log-concavity counterexample](ising-profile-log-concavity-counterexample-v1/README.md) — for one fixed five-up-spin slice of a periodic 4×3 grid, the exact counts 276, 60, 24 at 14, 16, 18 broken bonds violate log-concavity; one finite example only.

- [F₂ homology of three triangulated surfaces](math-at-f2-surface-homology-v1/README.md) — a small standard-library computation for explicit sphere, torus, and real-projective-plane meshes. Exact boundary ranks are cross-checked by image enumeration; the scope is only the three supplied finite complexes.

- [Weak Schur positive-anchor refutation](weak-schur-positive-anchors-v1/README.md) — a pinned DRAT proof shows that no Rowley extension to 208 can keep 65 particular old members from classes 1 and 3 in their original colors, even while every other integer and 208 may use any of the five colors. - [Local intersections of monomial branches](monomial-branch-intersections-v1/README.md) — the classical binomial-resultant formula checked against exact Macaulay ranks over `F₂`, `F₃`, and `F₅`, with a separate monomial-graph sweep of 1,701 small parameter tuples.
