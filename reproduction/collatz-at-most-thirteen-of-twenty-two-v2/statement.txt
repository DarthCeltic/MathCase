For every natural number n at least 3, if at most 13 of the first 22 accelerated Collatz inputs are odd, then after 22+h ordinary Collatz steps the value is strictly smaller than n, where h is the number of those odd inputs. The qualifying condition selects exactly 3,593,934 residue classes modulo 4,194,304; every n in each selected progression that is at least 3 has the stated decrease. This is bounded first descent, not a convergence result.
