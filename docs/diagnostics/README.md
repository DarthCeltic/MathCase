No exact M0 review or separate authorization was found in the reviewed record; keep it outside the featured theorem path. See the [package](../../reproduction/collatz-at-most-seventeen-of-twenty-seven-v1/README.md).
- **M31 step-34 sample:** a finite representative census and its input-byte binding record; it is not a class-wide theorem. See the [package](../../reproduction/m31-step34-crlf-binding-v1/README.md).
- **23/14 Collatz residue screen:** C++ and Python enumerate all 7,507,638 parity words with at most 14 odd inputs and agree on the exact minimum drop by odd count. A Lean kernel check covers masks 0 through 16,383 only. This is a computational cross-check of the existing bounded theorem, not a full Lean replay or a new result; the coarser coefficient-only attempt failed for odd counts 4 through 14. See the [methods, outputs, and limits](../../reproduction/collatz-23-14-residue-aware-screen-v1/README.md).
- **Collatz 24/15 residue screen (exploratory):** Python and C++ enumerate the 15,505,590 of 2^24 residue classes with at most 15 odd inputs in the first 24 accelerated steps. They agree on each of 16 odd-count totals and on the positive minimum affine margin in every row. This is a finite computation, not a Lean proof; the existing 24/15 Lean receipt remains exploratory and has no M0 review. See the [code, output, and scope limits](../../reproduction/collatz-24-15-residue-screen-v1/README.md).
- **Finite solver probes:** bounded outcomes such as `UNKNOWN` or a failed extension apply only to the stated formula, saved coloring, neighborhood, and time cap. They do not prove a general impossibility result. See the [frontier package](../../research/frontier-2026-11/README.md) and its [visitor page](https://darthceltic.github.io/mathcase/frontier.html).
